🌀 Typhoon readiness

Around 20 typhoons hit the Philippines every year. Preparation saves lives — start with your go-bag.

✅ Go-bag checklist (saves in your browser)

Before · Bago
  • Charge phones and power banks fully
  • Store 3 days of water (4L/person/day) and ready-to-eat food
  • Know your nearest evacuation center and safe route
  • Agree on a family meeting point and out-of-area contact
  • Monitor PAGASA advisories — don't rely on forwarded messages
During · Habang
  • Stay indoors, away from windows and glass
  • Never wade in floods — just 15cm of moving water can knock you down; it may be electrified
  • If told to evacuate, go early — before the storm peaks
  • Keep the radio on for official bulletins
After · Pagkatapos
  • Wait for the official "all clear" — the eye of the storm is not the end
  • Boil water 1–3 minutes before drinking if supply is unsure
  • Report downed power lines; never touch them
  • Check on neighbors, especially seniors and PWDs
